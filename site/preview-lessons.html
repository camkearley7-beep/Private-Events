Andrew McDonald Associate Professional, PGA of Canada

A certified PGA of Canada golf professional, Andrew has taught golf at every level for more than eight years, with personalized lessons built on technique, strategy and the mental game.

Known for his patient, supportive teaching style, he helps players build confidence and lower their scores. Beginner or advanced, every lesson is one-on-one on our Golfzon simulators, so you see exactly what the ball did.

What you'll work on

The whole game, not just the swing.

Personalized instruction across the three things that actually move your score.

  1. 01

    Technique

    Grip, setup and swing, built on what the ball is actually doing instead of guesswork.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    Club choice, course management and smarter targets, so good swings turn into lower scores.

  3. 03

    The mental game

    Routines and focus that hold up on the first tee and over the four-footer.
