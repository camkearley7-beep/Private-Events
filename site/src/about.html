Our story

It started as a joke.

Four Kearley siblings, an empty retail space next to the family insurance office, and one half-serious idea: what if we put golf simulators in there?

A year after that conversation, on January 31, The Bogey Club opened its doors. What began as “a bit of a passion project” became Newmarket's first luxurious indoor golf and lounge bar, a place to play real courses year round, rain or shine, with a cold drink never far away.

Today it's still family-run, with a full-time crew behind the bar and on the bays, and the same idea at its heart: golf is better when it's fun, social and open to everyone.

“Everybody's hungry to golf.”
