The Bogey Club · Homepage creative reset · Round 2

Three directions. One recommendation.

Each direction has its own idea, typeface, palette, photography and layout. None of them reuse anything from round one. Photo areas are shot briefs: no venue photography is invented.

Direction A · Recommended
Open Membership

A members' club where everyone is a member. Light, quiet and confident, with one red accent reserved for booking.

Instrument Sans · “The clubhouse, minus the rules.”

Direction B
After Hours

The club at 10 pm. Cinematic, low-light and amber, with golf as the excuse and the night as the product.

Inter Tight · “Golf until last call.”

Direction C
The Clubhouse Journal

The club as a printed journal: one serif family, captions and a contents list, closer to an apparel lookbook.

Newsreader · “Golf for the rest of us.”

Recommendation

Direction A, Open Membership.

It is the only direction that is light and breathable, distinctive, and fastest to book on a phone. Your logo already carries the loud, heavy condensed type. A quiet grotesk with one red accent lets the logo be the only loud thing on the page, instead of fighting it the way round one did. The idea (a members' club where everyone is a member) gives every section the same attitude without a single golf pun or graphic gimmick.

B is the strongest mood but the wrong default for a venue that also sells daytime golf, lessons and leagues. Its night photography could become A's events and karaoke imagery. C is beautiful but more heritage than rebellious.

Simplified homepage (Direction A): six sections
01HeroOne photograph. One headline. One sentence. Book a bay, plus Book karaoke and Plan an event as quiet secondary links.
02What's onGolf, Karaoke and Private events as a typographic index. On desktop the photo follows the line you point at; on mobile each is a full-width photo you tap.
03The venueTwo bars, four bays and cold beer on tap told in two photographs and three facts. No cards.
04Private eventsThe only colour-blocked section: one large image, three sentences and Start an event inquiry, which opens the existing form.
05Good to knowA compact list: price, how to book, hours (golf and bar labelled separately), address and one verified review.
06FooterNewsletter, plus every secondary link: lessons, leagues, Classic, shop, about, FAQs, contact, legal, social.

Mobile: Book a bay is the first button. Once you scroll, it lives in a floating dock with Karaoke, so both bookings are always one tap away. Plan an event sits in the hero and in its own section.
