Book your bay

  1. Pick a time online

    Choose your bay and time on our live booking calendar. Walk-ins are welcome when a bay is free, but booking ahead is best — especially evenings and weekends.

  2. Bring your crew

    Up to four players share a bay. Bigger group? Book more than one bay, or ask us about a private event.

  3. Tee it up

    Bring your own clubs or ask us about using ours. First time on a simulator? We'll get you set up.

  4. Stay for the 19th hole

    Grab a cold one at the bar during staffed bar hours, then decide whether it's one more round or karaoke.

How much does a simulator cost?
$60 per hour per bay, plus HST.
Is it per person or per bay?
Per bay. Split it between up to four players.
Are beginners welcome?
Absolutely. Golfzon works for first-timers and low handicappers alike.
When is the bar staffed?
During posted bar hours — see Visit or call us. Golf can be booked outside bar hours.

All FAQs
How do I book indoor golf?

Book online through our golf booking calendar. Walk-ins are welcome when a bay is open, but we recommend booking ahead for evenings and weekends.

How much does it cost to play?

$60 per hour per simulator bay, plus HST. Pricing is per bay, not per person, and up to four players can share a bay.

Can a big group golf together?

Yes. Each of our four bays fits up to four players, so you can book several bays side by side. For larger celebrations, send us an event inquiry.

Do I need my own golf clubs?

You're welcome to bring your own. If you don't have clubs, ask us when you book and we'll help you out.

How do I book karaoke?

Karaoke has its own booking — head to the karaoke page to reserve. Please don't use the golf calendar for karaoke.

Can I host a birthday or corporate event?

Yes. Our dedicated upstairs event space has its own full bar, and you can add golf simulators and karaoke to the night. Share your date, headcount and event type through our event inquiry and we'll follow up with options. More on our private events page.

How many guests can the event space hold?

It depends on the format of your event. Tell us your expected headcount and we'll recommend the right setup.

Is the bar always open?

No. Golf can be booked during golf hours, but drinks are only served by our licensed staff during staffed bar hours. You must be 19+ with valid ID to be served.

Do you offer golf lessons?

Yes — private lessons with our pro. Meet our pro to learn more and book.

Where is The Bogey Club?

17705 Leslie Street, Unit 5, Newmarket, Ontario L3Y 3E3 — close to Aurora and East Gwillimbury. Call (905) 235-4653 or email golf@thebogeyclub.com.
